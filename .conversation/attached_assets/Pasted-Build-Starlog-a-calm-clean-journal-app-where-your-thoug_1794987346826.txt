Build "Starlog": a calm, clean journal app where your thoughts live as stars in one big constellation. It's a place to write things down and organize your thoughts.

Stack: React + TypeScript frontend, Node/Express backend, Replit's built-in PostgreSQL database. Use HTML canvas (or a library like Konva or PixiJS) so everything stays smooth at 60fps, even with 500+ stars.

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Calm, clean and minimal. A soft deep-purple night sky with a gentle gradient and faint background stars. No clutter, no neon, nothing flashy.
- Very smooth: every movement uses soft easing (stars growing, cards opening, panning, zooming). Nothing jumps or snaps.
- Controls stay out of the way: a small toolbar that fades when you're not using it. One clean font (like Inter), lots of breathing room.
- Works with mouse and touch, on desktop and phone.

1. THE SKY
- Drag empty space to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom, smoothly.
- Faint background stars twinkle very slowly.

2. STARS
- Each star is a topic or thought. Create one by double-clicking (or long-pressing) empty space, or with a "+ New Star" button.
- Stars glow softly. Hovering brightens them a little.
- The more notes a star holds, the bigger it gets, growing gradually with a sensible maximum size.
- Drag any star to move it. Positions save automatically.

3. COLOR-CODING
- Users make their own color labels: a name plus a color from a calm, curated palette (e.g. "Work" = soft blue, "Ideas" = lavender, "Personal" = rose, "Goals" = gold).
- Any star can be given a label, and the color can be changed anytime.
- A small legend shows the labels. Clicking one highlights those stars and gently dims the rest.

4. THE JOURNAL CARD
- Clicking a star makes it grow slightly and opens a clean, translucent card beside it with a smooth animation.
- Each star has a title, its color label, and a list of notes like a journal thread.
- Every note is timestamped automatically (e.g. "Nov 17, 2026 · 11:29 PM"). Notes can be added, edited and deleted; edited notes show "edited" with the time.
- Autosave while typing. Esc or clicking the sky closes the card.

5. CONNECTIONS
- A "Connect" mode (or dragging from one star's edge to another) draws a soft, thin line between two related stars.
- Connected stars form constellations, which can be named; the name appears faintly on the sky.
- Click a line to remove it.

6. ORGANIZING AND FINDING THOUGHTS
- Search: matching stars glow while the rest dim.
- A timeline slider that replays the sky growing over time, with stars appearing in the order they were created.

7. ACCOUNTS AND PRIVACY
- Log in with Replit Auth so each person's sky is private.
- A "Try the demo" button that opens a sample sky (about 15 stars with notes, 4 color labels and 3 named constellations) without signing up.

8. EXTRAS
- Export the whole journal as Markdown or JSON.
- Keyboard shortcuts: N for a new star, Esc to close a card, / to search.

Build it in this order and test each part before moving on: the sky with smooth pan and zoom, creating and dragging stars, the journal card with timestamped notes, saving to the database, color labels, star size by note count, connections, search and timeline, accounts and demo mode, extras.
